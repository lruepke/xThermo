A dash marks a phase that is not present or a property the model does not provide. Single-phase
    fluid states, including supercritical ones, are reported in the liquid column. Volume fractions are the library's
    saturations; mass fractions are derived from them as volume fraction × phase density / bulk density. In
    multiphase states the bulk heat capacity is the frozen (phase-weighted) value without latent heat, and bulk
    viscosity and expansivity are not defined; “not computed” marks a placeholder of the library, not a physical
    zero. See Multiphase properties.






  About, licences and limitations
  
Model

  
The H₂O–NaCl correlations of Driesner & Heinrich (2007) and Driesner (2007) with the IAPS-84 or IAPWS-95
    water equation of state, exactly as in the native xThermo library: the same C++ code, compiled with Emscripten.
    In the automated verification of every build, phases, statuses and errors are identical to the native Linux build
    and the properties agree within 10⁻⁹ relative (tiny phase fractions and near-zero compressibilities within small
    absolute round-off).

  
Limitations

  
    
    
  • Domain about 0–1000 °C, 1–5000 bar, all salinities; outside it the library reports OutOfRange.
    • 
    
  • H-P-X can report NotConverged in three narrow regions (near the critical point of water, next to pure NaCl
      in the melting range, and boiling at salinities below about 10⁻⁸) rather than return an inaccurate state.
    • 
    
  • Results can differ in the last digits between platforms, in H-P-X up to about 10⁻⁷ relative. This is
      numerical, not a thermodynamic discontinuity.
    • 
    
  • Known issues of the published correlations apply. See Model and limitations.
