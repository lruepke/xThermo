Provenance of this copy
-----------------------
- Imported into the xThermo repository on 2022-01-05 by Zhikui Guo (commit 18578db). The source of the imported copy
  is not recorded. The original distribution site (http://www.en.tu-harburg.de/PROST) and the mirror cited in the
  xThermo bibliography are no longer available, so the copy cannot be compared with the 1998 distribution.
- The imported copy already contained additions that are not part of an ANSI C distribution of 1998:
    * CMakeLists.txt and .gitignore (xThermo build files);
    * in steam4.h, a structure "struct gzk { double x; double y; }" and repeated "#ifdef __cplusplus extern "C""
      guards around individual declarations.
  These were presumably made for xThermo before the import (inference; not recorded in the history).
- COPYING.LIB-2.0 was added on 2022-03-13 (7847554) and PROST4_TXT.htm on 2022-03-27 (de8ad98), both by Zhikui Guo.
  PROST4_TXT.htm carries the 1998 $Id line of PROST4.TXT.
